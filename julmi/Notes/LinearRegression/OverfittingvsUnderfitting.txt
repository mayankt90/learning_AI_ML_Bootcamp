🔹 Underfitting
Model is too simple to capture the underlying structure of the data.
Symptoms:
High training error (High Bias)
High validation/test error (High Variance)
Causes:
Model is too simple
Not enough features
Too much regularization
Solution:
Add more features or complexity, Reduce regularization

🔹 Overfitting
Model is too complex and learns noise from the training data.
Symptoms:
Low training error (Low Bias)
High validation/test error (High Variance)
Causes:
Too many features
Not enough training data
Lack of regularization
Solution:
Use regularization (L1/L2)
Use simpler models
Cross-validation
Get more data
